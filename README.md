# Fei Su, PhD

**Medical Imaging (MRI & 3D) · Biomedical Imaging · Computational Pathology · Spatial Biology · Molecular Imaging**

Medical- and pathology-trained researcher with a PhD in Biomedical Imaging from the **University of Technology Sydney (UTS)**. My work combines quantitative microscopy, tissue morphology, molecular measurements, and computational analysis to investigate biological heterogeneity and develop more interpretable biomedical imaging methods.

My established work spans experimental bioimaging, quantitative microscopy, and computational pathology. I am **actively expanding my portfolio in medical imaging**, particularly MRI and 3D image analysis, alongside interests in spatial omics, multimodal representation learning, and evidence-grounded AI for tissue analysis.

---

## Research interests

- **Medical imaging (current development focus):** MRI, 3D image processing, anatomical image interpretation, image segmentation, and multimodal image analysis.

- **Computational pathology:** histopathology, whole-slide imaging, pathology foundation models, and evaluation across magnifications.
- **Spatial and molecular biology:** quantitative relationships between tissue morphology, spatial organization, and molecular readouts.
- **Biomedical and molecular imaging:** fluorescence microscopy, lanthanide-based probes, quantitative image analysis, and imaging biomarkers.
- **Trustworthy biomedical AI:** external validation, uncertainty, calibration, reproducibility, and evidence-grounded workflows.

### Medical imaging projects — in progress

I am currently developing hands-on projects in **MRI and 3D medical image analysis** to complement my research background in biomedical imaging and pathology. Planned practical components include:

- **MRI processing:** NIfTI loading, image orientation, resampling, intensity normalization, and quality checks.
- **3D segmentation:** PyTorch / MONAI baselines, inference, and qualitative overlay visualization.
- **Rigorous evaluation:** subject-level data splits, Dice, Hausdorff distance (HD95), and error analysis.

*These are current learning and development goals, not completed projects or validated clinical systems. Repository links and measured results will be added as implementations become available.*
